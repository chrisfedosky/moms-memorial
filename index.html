Welcome to our tribute page for Rebecca Diane Fedosky, a loving and kind soul who has left an indelible mark on our lives. Here, we honor her memory through the contributions of those who loved her.

Text Poem

A time capsule in orbit for each of our spheres Set forever in motion, for each of us to bear. Distances of eons stretch wide out between us Still, connected by a thread that's seemed limitless. Once the one to whom some of us caved, Now one among the millions of waves. With willpower bent to ease the burden, Now the stubbornness has had to be rewritten. The loss is complex—as all things are— With regret, hurt, pain, love, and it all. For if life was painfully simple, You'd still be right here. But none of us would have learned of the many, many ways To make you stay. Forever. Meaningful.
